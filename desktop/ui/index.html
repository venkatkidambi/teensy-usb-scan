Recording and motion limits

100 Hz recording on the Teensy, with periodic verified transfers to this computer. Keep the app connected for long runs; all samples appear together when the run ends. Quasi-static reserves travel time with settling margin, plus each hold; sinusoidal reserves up to 10 s for endpoint settling or the serial STOP transfer and finishes at the last cycle endpoint. Start positions use the current encoder reference. Sinusoidal frequency: 0.1–10 Hz within the configured speed limit; tracking depends on the controller and load. Position resolution: 0.078 µm.

How to read these fits

Sinusoidal fit during the waveform only. Positive delay means the second signal lags; signed phase is wrapped to ±180° or ±π radians (±half a period). Host receipt means the Teensy USB host. Controller TIME has 0.1 ms units, but its association with EPOS acquisition is undocumented; no synchronization or latency correction is applied. Force uses the plotted recording time. These include motion, transport and sampling effects; fit R² measures sinusoidal agreement. Amplitudes are fitted center-to-peak values over the same time window: 100% matches the commanded amplitude, and positive error means a larger position amplitude. Phase is fitted independently, so a pure delay does not count as amplitude error.
