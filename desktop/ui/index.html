Test behavior

Each pulse requests one encoder count (0.078 µm). Verify the direction against encoder feedback. The encoder can differ from the target within the controller’s positioning tolerance. After the final pulse, ENABLE stays on for up to five seconds while fresh target and position-reached feedback is checked. Stop, a fault or the watchdog disables it. Last test counts are captured before restoring serial control; the live USB target can subsequently change. USB movement and experiment starts remain locked until GPIO finishes and serial control is restored. Stop cancels the test and retries restoration after a fault.

How to read phase delays

Sinusoidal fit during the waveform only. Positive delay means the second signal lags; signed phase is wrapped to ±180° or ±π radians (±half a period). Host receipt means the Teensy USB host. Force uses the plotted recording time. These include motion, transport and sampling effects; fit R² measures sinusoidal agreement.
