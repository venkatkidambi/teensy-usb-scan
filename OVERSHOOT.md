6. **Identity and ordering proof.** Each accepted waveform command supplies its TX sequence as the expected completion token. The existing connection epoch check rejects reconnects; sequence counters reset at run start after positioning drains. Idle can advance a stage only if both queued and completed sequences match the expected token. Old, repeated or future progress with a mismatched token faults and requests STOP. Detection clears pending ordinary intent before dispatch. NeedZero drains an already submitted ordinary packet and submits zero alone. AwaitZero submits direction only after matching completion. AwaitDirection commits direction and computes resume from actual time. AwaitResume waits for its matching completion, discards stale intent and schedules a fresh evaluation. No phase can dispatch ordinary SSPD. STOP/ABORT, disconnect, epoch change and final extremum preempt this transaction and clear its pending state. Completion after termination cannot restart waveform polling.

   This adds one per-run sequence token, not a queue. The real transport supplies progress; synchronous native doubles may use zero instrumentation. The proof assumes TinyUSB's existing one-completion-per-submitted-packet contract. Its untagged CDC callback cannot identify an arbitrary replay inside the lower driver that masquerades as completion of a newer outstanding packet. Idle duplicates and mismatched identities are covered; universal immunity to lower-driver corruption is not claimed. USB completion does not prove controller execution. A broad driver rewrite was not justified by this dataset.
